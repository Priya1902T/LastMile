# Validation before building

Find 5 junior or mid-level people with an interview in the next 7 days. Run intake and triage by hand in a chat, with no app.

Questions to answer:
1. Did they finish the intake without dropping off?
2. Did they say the plan felt specific to them?
3. Did they come back on day 2 or ask for a mock?
4. Would they pay, and how much?

If fewer than 3 of 5 say yes to the second and third, narrow the audience or rethink before building.
